About the Shiba Inu

The Shiba Inu is a breed of hunting dog from Japan. A small-to-medium breed, it is the smallest of the six original dog breeds native to Japan.

The Shiba Inu has been identified as a basal breed that predates the emergence of the modern breeds in the 19th century.
