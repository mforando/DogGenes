About the Kerry Blue Terrier

The Kerry Blue Terrier or Irish Blue Terrier is an Irish breed of dog of terrier type.

The Kerry Blue is first documented in 1847, when it was described as bluish slate in colour, marked with darker blotches and patches, and often with black about the legs and muzzle.
