About the Greater Swiss Mountain Dog

The Greater Swiss Mountain Dog is a dog breed which was developed in the Swiss Alps.

The origin of the Greater Swiss Mountain Dog is not known. Beginning in 1515, the remote valleys of Switzerland were more or less isolated from world history for three centuries.
