Breed Explorer

18,006 dogs, one wall

Every photo in the Dog CEO collection: 18,006 pictures of 161 breeds, plus mixed breeds and a few wild cousins, gathered into one family portrait. Breeds that share a branch of the family tree cluster together, and the oldest lineages sit closest to the grey wolf in the middle. Rearrange the wall by family group, original job, region, or photo color, or send every dog home on the map. Hover over a photo to see its breed and click to open it.

Photos: the Dog CEO API, built on the Stanford Dogs dataset, shown as small thumbnails and loaded at full size when opened. Families come from Parker et al. 2017 for the breeds it sampled. Jobs and origins come from standard breed histories. “Photo color” sorts by the average color at the center of each photo, which is usually the dog but sometimes the grass.
